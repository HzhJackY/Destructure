# INC-003 — Terminal Boundary Regression

## Symptom

Complete tables were blocked or truncated at the first total.

## Root causes

Treating any TOTAL as hard end, requiring a following same-level heading, and losing post-total memo or secondary classification blocks.

## Permanent invariant

A total may be local, intermediate, section, or final. Closure depends on structure, reconciliation, continuation, contamination, and container evidence.
